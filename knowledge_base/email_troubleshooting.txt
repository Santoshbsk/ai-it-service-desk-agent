IT SUPPORT RUNBOOK

Title: Corporate Email Troubleshooting
Document ID: IT-EMAIL-001

Symptoms:
- Email is not synchronizing
- Outlook is not opening
- New emails are not appearing

Troubleshooting Steps:

1. Verify internet connectivity.
2. Restart Outlook.
3. Confirm that Outlook is not in Offline mode.
4. Restart the laptop.
5. Check whether the issue occurs in webmail.
6. If webmail works but Outlook does not, escalate to Desktop Support.

Priority:
Email issues that prevent critical business communication may require High priority handling.
