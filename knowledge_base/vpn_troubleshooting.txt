IT SUPPORT RUNBOOK

Title: VPN Connection Troubleshooting
Document ID: IT-VPN-001

Symptoms:
- Unable to connect to corporate VPN
- VPN connection repeatedly disconnects
- VPN authentication fails

Troubleshooting Steps:

1. Verify that the employee has an active internet connection.
2. Confirm that the corporate VPN client is running.
3. Restart the VPN client.
4. Verify that the employee is using the correct corporate credentials.
5. If multi-factor authentication fails, verify that the employee's MFA method is working.
6. If the VPN still cannot connect, restart the laptop and try again.
7. If the issue persists after these steps, escalate the incident to the Network Support team.

Priority:
VPN issues preventing an employee from accessing critical business applications should be treated as High priority.
